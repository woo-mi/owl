## Blank plush heads for v3 customization

These three assets were edited with the built-in image generation tool from their corresponding existing blue Soft Owl images. The edit removes the face patch, eyes, and beak while preserving the blue plush head shape, texture, camera angle, and alpha transparency. They provide the base heads for composing alternate facial features in v3.
