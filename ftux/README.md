# Indexing

Independent copy of Full-Page Workspace Layout Including Goals, based on commit
ef50a13c2a63ac4709d26a87dfda12d10285fe86.

Live URL: https://woo-mi.github.io/owl/indexing/

Design source: “indexing content” (S2Qem) in Owl MVP (Kapil&Alonzo).

The conversation starts at the NEW divider with weekly pipeline reviews, business metrics,
ARR definitions, pipeline health, and September revenue analysis. The top-right
index links to those conversation topics and the two shared artifacts. The copied
full-page workspace and Goals layout versions remain available through the Owl
identity header. Goal edits are stored separately for this prototype.

The left tick navigator uses the older Owl pagination interaction, with seven
stops for the five Recent topics and the two shared artifacts. Hover or focus a
tick to preview it, click to jump, and use the bookmark button to save a stop.
Arrow keys and Home/End browse the stops; Escape closes the preview. Artifact
previews also provide the external App or Chat link. Bookmarks are stored only
for this Indexing prototype.

Connections remain available through Manage in the index menu. Tool connections,
app previews, and replies are local prototype interactions; they do not connect
accounts or create external apps. Artifact links retain their existing external destinations.
