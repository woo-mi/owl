# FTUX

Live URL: https://woo-mi.github.io/owl/ftux/

The onboarding conversation matches “FTUX real” (KJKJh) in Owl MVP (Kapil&Alonzo).
It replaces the former starter-prompt flow. Introductory bubbles appear in sequence,
then show Notion, Gmail, Calendar, Slack, and Gong connection buttons. Connecting a
context tool or choosing Skip for now reveals the Monday pipeline-review suggestion.
The user chooses Yes (or sends an affirmative reply) to reveal HubSpot and Salesforce.
Connecting a CRM reveals the Weekly Sales Pipeline Review app link and preview.
Skipping CRM leaves the connection options available without claiming to build the app.

Connections are local prototype states, initiated only by clicking Connect. New Chat
resets the conversation and connections. The index and right-side ticks reflect only
revealed conversation topics, and the app appears in Artifacts after its reveal.
The existing workspace, Owl customization, and animated bubble timing remain available.
The app preview uses the destination already supplied by the Pen design; no real account
connection or app generation occurs in this prototype.
